Our Training Programs
01
Fall Protection

Fall Protection Training

Build essential awareness for working safely around fall hazards and understand the fundamentals of fall protection systems used on today's jobsites.

Focus

Fall hazards, protection systems, equipment awareness, safe work practices, and prevention.

02
Competent Person

24 Hour Competent Person Regular Fall Protection

Develop the knowledge and skills needed to support regular fall protection activities, recognize hazards, and understand competent person responsibilities on the jobsite.

Focus

Competent person responsibilities, fall protection systems, hazard recognition, and jobsite practices.

03
Rescue Training

Self Rescue for Fall Protection

Prepare workers to understand self-rescue procedures, emergency response considerations, and critical response steps following a fall protection incident.

Focus

Self-rescue concepts, emergency response, fall incidents, and rescue preparedness.

04
Refresher Training

24 Hour Fall Protection Refresher

Refresh essential fall protection knowledge and reinforce safe practices for professionals who need continued awareness and readiness on the jobsite.

Focus

Fall protection review, hazard awareness, safe practices, system knowledge, and continued jobsite readiness.

05
Core Safety

OSHA Safety Training

Build a stronger safety foundation with practical OSHA-focused training designed for today's demanding worksites.

Focus

OSHA awareness, workplace hazards, compliance fundamentals, and safer daily work practices.

Our Training Process

A Better Way to Train.

Safety training should be more than a presentation. Our process takes your team from understanding the risk to confidently applying what they learned on the jobsite.

Assess • Train • Practice • Perform
01
Step One

Assess

We identify your team's needs, jobsite risks, existing practices, and training gaps.

02
Step Two

Train

Clear, practical instruction focused on the hazards workers actually face in the field.

03
Step Three

Practice

Turn knowledge into action through practical examples, discussions, and real-world scenarios.

04
Step Four

Certify

Confirm understanding and provide your team with a clear path forward after training.

05
Step Five

Perform

Take the training back to the jobsite and put safer decisions into practice every day.
